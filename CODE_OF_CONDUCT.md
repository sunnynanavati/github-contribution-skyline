# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Be considerate and constructive, respect differing experiences, give technical feedback in good faith, and protect private information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may remove content or participation that violates this policy.
